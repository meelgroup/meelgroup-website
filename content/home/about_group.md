Welcome to the Meel Group’s web page. Our group is based at the [University of Toronto](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/) and the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.scs.gatech.edu/).

Led by [Kuldeep S. Meel](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~meel/), we develop scalable automated reasoning techniques that bridge theory and practice. Our research draws on randomized algorithms, statistical inference, formal methods, and distribution testing.

<h3>Interests</h3>

- Formal Methods
- Databases
- Knowledge Representation and Reasoning
- Constrained Sampling and Counting
