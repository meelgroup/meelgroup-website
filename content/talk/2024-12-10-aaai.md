We have three papers accepted to AAAI-25.

The [first paper][paper1] takes a step towards real-time approximate model counting. Joint work with Jiong Yang and Yash Pote.

The [second paper][paper2] focuses on probabilistic explanations for linear models. Joint work with Bernardo Subercaseaux and Marcelo Arenas.

The [third paper][paper3] focuses on projected and incremental pseudo-boolean model counting. Joint work with Suwei Yang.
