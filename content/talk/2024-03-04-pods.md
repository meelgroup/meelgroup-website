We have two papers accepted to [PODS-24][pods].<br/>
1. The [first paper][paper1], co-authored with [Sourav Chakraborty][sourav] and [Umang Mathur][umang] presents a faster FPRAS for counting the number of words accepted by NFA.

2. The [second paper][paper2], co-authored with [Pavan Aduri][pavan],[Sourav Chakraborty][sourav], and [N.V. Vinodchandran][vinod] introduces the model of right to be forgotten in the context of streaming.
