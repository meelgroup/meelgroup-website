Two of our papers were accepted to [LPAR-26](https://easychair.org/smart-program/LPAR-26/index.html), held in Spetses, Greece, on October 25–30, 2026:

- **Scaling Zero Knowledge UNSAT Verification via Normalized Chaining** — Ashwin Karthikeyan, Ethan Kharitonov, Kuldeep S. Meel, and Anwar Hithnawi. The work improves the scalability of zero-knowledge UNSAT certification by normalizing resolution proofs to reduce prover memory requirements.
- **Formally Solving Answer-Construction Problems in Lean** — Jialiang Sun, Yuzhi Tang, Ao Li, Chris Maddison, and Kuldeep Meel. The work introduces Enumerate-Conjecture-Prove, combining answer construction with machine-checked proofs and admissibility checks in Lean.

See the [official accepted-paper list and abstracts](https://easychair.org/smart-program/LPAR-26/accepted-detailed.html).
