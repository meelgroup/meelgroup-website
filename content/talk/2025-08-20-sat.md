Four of our papers are accepted to SAT 2025:
- [CNFs and DNFs with Exactly k Solutions][paper1] (with L. Sunil Chandran and Rishikesh Gajjala).
- [Towards Practical First-Order Model Counting][paper2] (with Ananth K. Kidambi, Guramrit Singh, and Paulius Dilkas).
- [On Top-Down Pseudo-Boolean Model Counting][paper3] (with Suwei Yang and Yong Lai).
- [Efficient Certified Reasoning for Binarized Neural Networks][paper4] (with Jiong Yang, Yong Kiam Tan, Mate Soos, and Magnus O. Myreen).
