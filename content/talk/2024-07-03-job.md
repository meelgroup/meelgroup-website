Four of our advisees were in job market this past academic year and all of them managed to secure tenure-track positions in four countries across three continents. Congratulations!

1. [Teodora Baluta][teo] (PhD, co-advised with [Prateek Saxena][prateek]) will be joining Georgia Tech as an Assistant Professor. <br/>
2. [Gunjan Kumar][gunjan] (post-doc, co-advised with [Diptarka Chakraborty][diptarka]) will be joining IIT Kanpur as an Assistant Professor. <br/>
3. [Anna Latour][anna] (post-doc) will be joining TU Delft as an Assistant Professor. <br/>
4. [Tim van Bremen][tim] (post-doc) will be joining Nanyang Technical University (NTU) Singapore as an Assistant Professor.
